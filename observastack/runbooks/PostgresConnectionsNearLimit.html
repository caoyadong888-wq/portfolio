# PostgresConnectionsNearLimit

Larkspur Grocers is fictional. This note explains the alert. It is not an incident report. It does not record a page, a customer impact, or an MTTR.

## Signal

- Severity: `warning`
- Team: `platform`
- Pending duration (`for`): 10m

```promql
pg_stat_activity_connections / pg_settings_max_connections > 0.85
```

## What the condition means

Connections in use divided by max_connections have stayed above 0.85 for 10 minutes. The series name is the catalog name pg_stat_activity_connections.
